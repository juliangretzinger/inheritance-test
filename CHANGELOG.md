# Changelog

Versions are frozen once externally cited; assessments state the version they were made under.

## v1.1 — 2026-07-12

- Verdict renamed: "right structure, wrong claim" → **"right structure, wrong label"** (the defect is in how the instrument is described, not in a legal claim; "label" is the more precise term and pairs with the disclosure remedy).
- Scope sentence made explicit in the verdict section: a right-wrapper finding is a statement about form, not about the merits, solvency, or authenticity of the underlying.
- No change to the three gates, the five-verdict structure, or the procedure.

## v1.0 — 2026-07-10

Initial public release, published simultaneously at
https://juliangretzinger.com/inheritance-test.html (canonical) and archived
with DOI via Zenodo.

- The axiom and the true-underlying (pool) clarification
- Two wrapper families: packaging vs registration
- Scope: wrapper risk only; not-applicable handling for unwrapped direct ownership
- Three gates, incl. inherited-vs-provided liquidity and the
  protected-entitlement vs unsecured-claim distinction
- The claim-conversion rule
- Verdicts assigned against a claim-set; five-finding taxonomy
- Six worked cases (classical and tokenised, one to four layers,
  passing and failing)
- Operating instruction v1.0: verify-first, indeterminate-when-unknown
